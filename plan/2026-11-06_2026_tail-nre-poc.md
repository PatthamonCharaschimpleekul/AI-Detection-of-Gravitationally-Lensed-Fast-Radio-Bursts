# Plan: 2-day POC, tail-calibrated neural likelihood ratio

- **Date/time:** 2026-11-06

## What we will do
Test the proposed A* method idea cheaply, before any commitment. A burst pair is either lensed (same intrinsic structure) or an intrinsic repeat (correlation rho < 1).

1. Build a linear-Gaussian simulator of 1-D burst pairs where the exact marginal likelihood ratio (LR) is known in closed form.
2. Train a Siamese encoder with feature-map fusion (Project_Description Sec. 6.3) as a neural ratio estimator, comparing:
   - `plain`: negatives drawn from the prior
   - `naive`: hard negatives oversampled, no correction (what Sec. 6.3 currently describes)
   - `iw`: hard negatives oversampled, importance-weighted loss
   - `cond`: conditioned on rho, then marginalised over the prior
3. Run each with unlimited simulation and with a finite pool of 4539 templates (Catalog 2 size).
4. Check the noise shortcut on real CHIME waterfalls.

## Fixed before seeing any network result
Simulator priors may be adjusted only using exact-LR statistics, never network results.
rho = 0, .3, .6, .8, .9, .95, .98 with prior .40, .25, .15, .10, .06, .03, .01. Hard-negative proposal: uniform over rho.

| Gate | Pass condition | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| G0 correctness | E_H0[r] = 1 and E_H1[1/r] = 1 within Monte Carlo error; exact LR is calibrated | Bug: stop and fix |
| G1 a problem exists | `plain` at exact LR >= 1e3: abs. bias or RMSE > 0.3 dex, or false-positive rate at r >= 1e3 off by > 2x from exact, and this survives a bigger model and temperature scaling | Tail-calibration thesis is dead: pivot to the science benchmark paper |
| G2 naive is biased | `naive` false-positive rate or recall at r >= 1e3 off by > 2x from exact | Sec. 6.3 contradiction is not important in practice |
| G3 a fix works | `iw` or `cond` cuts tail RMSE by >= 30% vs both `plain` and `naive`, with seed ranges not overlapping | No method contribution yet |
| G4 shortcut is real | Naive copy-paste pairs are separable from real repeat pairs by off-pulse noise alone; re-injected pairs are not | Downgrade the risk |

## Expected result
Honest prior guess: G0 passes, G2 passes, G1 and G3 uncertain. G1 is the real risk: with unlimited simulation a small network may be accurate enough.

## Risks / open questions
- A weak network can fake a tail problem. Mitigation: report excess cross-entropy over the exact LR and rerun a larger model.
- A 1-D Gaussian benchmark is not real data. A POC pass justifies more work; it is not a paper result.
- No time shifts, no time-order asymmetry, no source clustering in this POC.
