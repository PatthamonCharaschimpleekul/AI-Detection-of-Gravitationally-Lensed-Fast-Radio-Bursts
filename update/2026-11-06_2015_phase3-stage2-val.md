## Dead runs
- `m4_s1` and all three model-7 runs sit at loss 1.3863 = ln 4 from about step 2000, with AUC 0.5.
- **Cause, checked on saved checkpoints:** the first ReLU layer of the classification head is dead. Active units out of 128: `m4_s1` 0, `m7_s0` 0, `m7_s1` 10, `m7_s2` 1, with logit spread ≤ 1e-4 across pairs. Working runs have 20–76 active units (`m4_s0` only 20, so it is close to the same failure).
- These are optimisation failures, not evidence about the methods. **Model 7 must not be reported as worse than model 6 from these runs.** The checkpoints are from about step 1000–2000 of a failed run.
- Why the units die (likely large early updates at the full 1e-3 rate, no warmup) is not tested.

## Grouped-fold trigger
- CI half-width of the primary difference: **0.144** (> 0.10). 5 × slowest model-6 run: **5.5 GPU-h** (≤ 24). **Triggered.**
- By the agreed rule, the primary changes to 5 source-grouped out-of-fold scores before the test is opened. This needs a plan addendum and bright sets per fold, with training settings frozen first.
